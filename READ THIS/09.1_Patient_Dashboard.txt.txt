09.1_Patient_Dashboard.txt

Project Name

AI Oncology Patient Intelligence Platform

Feature Name

Patient Home Space (Patient Dashboard)

Document Purpose

This document defines the Patient Home Space. It explains what the patient sees immediately after signing in, the available actions, and the expected behaviour of every component.

--------------------------------------------------

1. Purpose

Patient Home Space is the patient's arrival point in the environment, at Depth 1.

Its purpose is to help the patient quickly understand what they need to do and to provide effortless access to every important part of their record.

It is not a dashboard of widgets. It is a calm, continuous surface that answers one question: what is the next thing this patient needs.

The space remains simple and easy to understand.

Medical terminology is minimized wherever possible.

The patient must never feel overwhelmed.

--------------------------------------------------

2. Target User

Patient

--------------------------------------------------

3. Objectives

Provide a clear overview of the patient's account.

Display pending tasks.

Display recent activity.

Display notifications.

Provide quick access to reports.

Provide quick access to the patient's timeline.

Provide quick access to notes from the oncologist.

--------------------------------------------------

4. Contextual Destinations

Reachable from Patient Home Space through spatial movement, without a sidebar and without nested menus:

Journey (Timeline)

Evidence (Reports)

Actions (Tasks)

Guidance (Notes)

Signals (Notifications)

Account (Profile and Settings)

Continuous Return always brings the patient back to Patient Home Space.

--------------------------------------------------

5. Spatial Composition

Patient Home Space is presented as a single continuous surface, in priority order:

Welcome and orientation

Quick Summary

Assigned Actions

Recent Evidence

Recent Guidance

Journey Preview

Signals

The most urgent information appears first and closest to the viewer. Less urgent information sits further back and is revealed as the patient moves down through the space.

Entering any item zooms into the space that item belongs to.

--------------------------------------------------

6. Welcome and Orientation

Display:

Patient Name

Greeting

Current Date

--------------------------------------------------

7. Quick Summary

Display:

Total Reports

Pending Tasks

Completed Tasks

Recent Upload

These are presented as a single continuous summary line rather than as separate boxes.

--------------------------------------------------

8. Assigned Actions (Tasks)

Display all pending tasks assigned by the oncologist.

Each task should display:

Task Title

Short Description

Assigned Date

Due Date (if available)

Current Status

Upload control (if required)

View Details

Uploading happens in place. The patient never leaves the space to complete a task.

--------------------------------------------------

9. Recent Evidence (Recent Uploads)

Display recently uploaded reports.

Each report should display:

Report Name

Report Type

Upload Date

Processing Status

View Report

Selecting a report opens it in Focus above the current space.

--------------------------------------------------

10. Journey Preview (Timeline Preview)

Display the latest timeline events.

Each event should display:

Date

Title

Short Description

View Timeline

Selecting the preview zooms into the full Journey.

--------------------------------------------------

11. Recent Guidance (Patient Notes)

Display notes written by the oncologist.

Each note should display:

Date

Title

Message

Read More

Only notes intended for the patient should appear.

--------------------------------------------------

12. Signals (Notifications)

Display recent notifications.

Examples:

Task Assigned

Report Uploaded Successfully

New Note Available

Task Completed

Signals never interrupt the patient. Selecting a Signal moves the patient directly to the relevant space.

--------------------------------------------------

13. Search

Patients should be able to search their own reports through the Intent Bar.

Patients cannot search other patients.

--------------------------------------------------

14. Motion

Arrival into Patient Home Space is a descent from the Entry.

Entering any destination uses spatial zoom, with the selected item transforming into the space entered.

Newly arrived Signals and completed uploads are revealed with motion that draws attention to the change.

Reduced motion replaces spatial transitions with immediate cross-fades without any loss of information.

--------------------------------------------------

15. Empty States

No Reports

Display upload prompt.

No Tasks

Display informative message.

No Notes

Display informative message.

No Notifications

Display informative message.

--------------------------------------------------

16. Error States

Upload Failed

Network Error

Session Expired

Permission Denied

Display clear instructions for recovery. An error never breaks the surrounding space.

--------------------------------------------------

17. User Actions

Upload Report

View Reports

Open Timeline

View Tasks

Open Notes

View Notifications

Edit Profile

Open Settings

Logout

--------------------------------------------------

18. Business Rules

Patients can upload reports.

Patients cannot modify uploaded reports.

Patients cannot delete reports.

Patients cannot access AI processing details.

Patients cannot access private clinical notes.

Patients cannot assign tasks.

Patients cannot edit timeline events.

--------------------------------------------------

19. Performance Requirements

The space should load quickly.

Summary information should appear immediately.

Large report lists should support pagination.

The interface should remain responsive.

The structure of the space appears before its detail. A blank screen is never displayed.

--------------------------------------------------

20. Accessibility

Support keyboard navigation. Every spatial action has a keyboard equivalent.

Maintain readable typography.

Maintain proper spacing.

Provide meaningful labels for every interactive element.

Respect reduced motion.

--------------------------------------------------

21. Completion Criteria

Patient Home Space is complete when:

Patients can access every approved destination.

Assigned tasks are displayed.

Recent uploads are displayed.

Timeline preview is displayed.

Patient notes are displayed.

Notifications are displayed.

Navigation is spatial and consistent.

The space follows the approved UI/UX guidelines.

The space is ready for backend integration.
