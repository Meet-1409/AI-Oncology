09.1_Patient_Dashboard.txt

Project Name

AI Oncology Patient Intelligence Platform

Feature Name

Patient Dashboard

Document Purpose

This document defines the Patient Dashboard. It explains what the patient sees immediately after logging in, the available actions, and the expected behaviour of every component.

--------------------------------------------------

1. Purpose

The Patient Dashboard is the patient's home page.

Its purpose is to help the patient quickly understand what actions they need to complete and provide easy access to every important feature.

The dashboard should remain simple and easy to understand.

Medical terminology should be minimized wherever possible.

--------------------------------------------------

2. Target User

Patient

--------------------------------------------------

3. Objectives

Provide a clear overview of the patient's account.

Display pending tasks.

Display recent activity.

Display notifications.

Provide quick access to reports.

Provide quick access to the patient's timeline.

Provide quick access to notes from the oncologist.

Title

Display notes written by the oncologist.

13. Search

Patients should be able to search their own reports.

Patients cannot search other patients.

--------------------------------------------------

--------------------------------------------------

17. Business Rules

Patients can upload reports.

Patients cannot modify uploaded reports.

Patients cannot delete reports.

Patients cannot access AI processing details.

Patients cannot access private clinical notes.

Patients cannot assign tasks.

Patients cannot edit timeline events.

--------------------------------------------------

18. Performance Requirements

Dashboard should load quickly.

Summary cards should appear immediately.

Large report lists should support pagination.

The interface should remain responsive.

--------------------------------------------------

19. Accessibility

Support keyboard navigation.

Maintain readable typography.

Maintain proper spacing.

Provide meaningful labels for every interactive element.

--------------------------------------------------

20. Completion Criteria

The Patient Dashboard is complete when:

Patients can access every approved module.

Assigned tasks are displayed.

Recent uploads are displayed.

Timeline preview is displayed.

Patient notes are displayed.

Notifications are displayed.

Navigation is consistent.

The dashboard follows the approved UI/UX guidelines.

The dashboard is ready for backend integration.
