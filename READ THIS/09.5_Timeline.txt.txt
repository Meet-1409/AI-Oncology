09.5_Timeline.txt

Project Name

AI Oncology Patient Intelligence Platform

Feature Name

Journey (Timeline)

Document Purpose

This document defines the Journey. It represents the patient's complete cancer journey in chronological order. Every important medical event appears here and remains permanently linked to the Patient Case.

--------------------------------------------------

1. Purpose

The Journey provides a complete chronological view of the patient's cancer journey.

It allows the oncologist to quickly understand what happened, when it happened, and how the patient's condition changed over time.

The Journey is both a view and a control. Moving through it moves the entire Patient Space through time, including the Body.

It is presented as a continuous path through time rather than a list of rows.

5. Spatial Composition

The Journey is a continuous path along Patient Space.

Events are positioned by their true position in time, so gaps and clusters in treatment are immediately visible.

Density of information increases as the oncologist moves closer to a period of time. Distant periods show only significant events; approaching a period reveals its detail.

The latest event is the default position.

Event detail opens in Focus without leaving Patient Space.

--------------------------------------------------

13. Journey and Reports

Every report automatically appears in the Journey.

Selecting a report event opens the related report in Focus.

--------------------------------------------------

14. Journey and Patient Intelligence

Journey events link to related AI summaries whenever available.

The original report always remains accessible.

--------------------------------------------------

15. Journey and the Body

The Journey synchronizes with the Body.

Selecting a clinical date updates the Body to display the patient's condition for that date.

Moving through the Journey updates the Body continuously.

When comparing two dates, the Journey highlights both selected events.

--------------------------------------------------

16. Motion

Moving through time is continuous, and the Body updates in step with the movement.

Approaching a period of time progressively reveals its detail.

Selecting an event lifts it into Focus while the Journey remains visible behind it.

Reduced motion presents time changes directly, without continuous interpolation. All information remains identical.

18. Business Rules

Timeline events must remain chronological.

Historical events must never be deleted.

Uploaded reports automatically become timeline events.

Tasks automatically become timeline events.

Patient Notes automatically become timeline events.

Private Clinical Notes should not appear in the patient timeline.

--------------------------------------------------
