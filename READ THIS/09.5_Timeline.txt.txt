09.5_Timeline.txt

Project Name

AI Oncology Patient Intelligence Platform

Feature Name

Timeline

Document Purpose

This document defines the Timeline module. The Timeline represents the patient's complete cancer journey in chronological order. Every important medical event should appear here and remain permanently linked to the Patient Case.

--------------------------------------------------

1. Purpose

The Timeline provides a complete chronological view of the patient's cancer journey.

It should allow the oncologist to quickly understand what happened, when it happened, and how the patient's condition changed over time.

--------------------------------------------------

2. Target Users

Oncologist

--------------------------------------------------

13. Timeline and Reports

Every report should automatically appear in the Timeline.

Selecting a report event should open the related report.

--------------------------------------------------

14. Timeline and Patient Intelligence

Timeline events should link to related AI summaries whenever available.

The original report should always remain accessible.

--------------------------------------------------

15. Timeline and Digital Twin

Timeline should synchronize with the Digital Twin.

Selecting a clinical date should allow the Digital Twin to display the patient's condition for that date.

When comparing two dates, the Timeline should highlight both selected events.

17. Business Rules

Timeline events must remain chronological.

Historical events must never be deleted.

Uploaded reports automatically become timeline events.

Tasks automatically become timeline events.

Patient Notes automatically become timeline events.

Private Clinical Notes should not appear in the patient timeline.
