03_Frontend_App_Flow.txt

Project Name

AI Oncology Patient Intelligence Platform

Document Purpose

This document defines the complete frontend flow of the application. Every space, spatial transition, control action and user journey must follow this document. Developers must not change the navigation or workflow unless instructed by the Product Owner.

The application is one continuous environment. The flows below describe movement through that environment, not navigation between unrelated pages. All vocabulary, depth levels and motion rules are defined in 04_UI_UX_Design_Guide.

--------------------------------------------------

1. User Roles

There are only two user roles.

Patient

Oncologist

Every space belongs to one of these roles.

--------------------------------------------------

2. Depth Model

The environment has four depth levels.

Depth 0 - Entry (Landing Page and authentication)

Depth 1 - Home Space (Dashboard)

Depth 2 - Patient Space (Patient Profile), centred on the Body

Depth 3 - Focus (a single report, event, task, note or organ, opened in place)

Moving deeper zooms in. Moving back zooms out. The space behind remains visible and recognisable. Depth never exceeds four levels.

--------------------------------------------------

3. Entry (Public Website)

The Entry is a cinematic introduction to the system.

It presents, as one continuous experience:

Introduction

About

Features

How It Works

Contact

Access to authentication

The Entry communicates product identity, quality and trust immediately.

No patient information is accessible without authentication.

--------------------------------------------------

4. Authentication Flow

Entry

↓

Sign in

↓

Enter credentials

↓

Authentication

↓

Descend into Home Space based on role

Patient

↓

Patient Home Space

Oncologist

↓

Practice Space

If authentication fails

↓

Display error in place

↓

Remain at the sign in step

Authentication is a descent into the environment, not a jump to an unrelated page. The Entry remains behind the user.

--------------------------------------------------

5. Patient Environment

Patient Home Space (Depth 1)

Contextual destinations reachable from Patient Home Space:

Journey (Timeline)

Evidence (Reports)

Actions (Tasks)

Guidance (Notes)

Signals (Notifications)

Account (Profile and Settings)

The patient always returns to Patient Home Space using Continuous Return.

The patient reaches every destination without a persistent sidebar and without nested menus.

--------------------------------------------------

6. Oncologist Environment

Practice Space (Depth 1)

Contextual destinations reachable from Practice Space:

Patient List

Signals (Notifications)

Account (Profile and Settings)

Entering a patient descends into Patient Space (Depth 2).

Inside Patient Space, the Body sits at the centre. The Contextual Orbit provides:

Journey (Timeline)

Evidence (Reports)

Understanding (Patient Intelligence)

Actions (Tasks)

Guidance (Notes)

The oncologist reaches every destination without a persistent sidebar and without nested menus.

--------------------------------------------------

7. Patient Workflow

Patient opens the application.

↓

Entry.

↓

Sign in.

↓

Patient Home Space appears.

↓

Patient sees assigned Actions immediately.

↓

If an Action requires a report upload

↓

Patient uploads report in place.

↓

Upload completes.

↓

Patient receives confirmation in place.

↓

Journey updates.

↓

Patient reads Guidance from the oncologist.

↓

Sign out.

--------------------------------------------------

↓

↓

Descend into Patient Space. The selected patient becomes the space entered.

The Body appears at the centre with the current clinical state.

↓

Review Understanding.

↓

Review Journey.

↓

Review Evidence.

↓

Review the Body.

↓

Assign Actions if needed.

↓

↓

Write Private Observations.

↓

Save.

↓

Sign out.

--------------------------------------------------

9. Home Space Flow

Home Space presents, in priority order:

Patient Overview

Recent Activity

Signals

Pending Actions

Recent Evidence

Home Space provides access to every major destination through spatial movement rather than a menu.

Selecting any item zooms into the space that item belongs to.

--------------------------------------------------

10. Patient Space Flow

Patient List

↓

Select Patient

↓

Patient Space

↓

Body at centre

Contextual Orbit provides:

Patient Information

Journey

Evidence

Understanding

Actions

Guidance

Patient identity remains visible at all times inside Patient Space.

Moving from one patient to another is a lateral movement and must feel continuous.

↓

Upload validation.

↓

Upload complete.

↓

Processing status shown in place.

↓

↓

Journey updates.

↓

AI processing begins.

↓

↓

The Body and Understanding update.

--------------------------------------------------

12. Journey Flow (Timeline)

Journey opens.

↓

Clinical events appear in chronological order.

↓

Select event.

↓

Event opens in Focus.

↓

Related report is reachable from Focus.

↓

Evidence available.

↓

Return to Journey by zooming out.

The Journey is also a control. Moving through the Journey moves the entire Patient Space through time.

--------------------------------------------------

13. Body Flow (Digital Twin)

Enter the Body.

↓

Model loads progressively.

↓

Rotate.

↓

Zoom.

↓

Pan.

↓

Select a clinical date on the Journey.

↓

Model updates continuously.

↓

Compare with a previous date.

↓

Evidence panel updates.

Selecting an organ reveals the clinical information and supporting evidence for that organ in place.

Disease Progression.

↓

↓

↓

Write observation.

↓

Save.

↓

--------------------------------------------------

18. Signals (Notifications)

Patient Signals

New task.

Task completed.

Report uploaded.

New patient note.

Oncologist Signals

Patient uploaded report.

Patient completed task.

AI processing completed.

System alerts.

Selecting a Signal moves the user directly into the relevant space.

Signals never interrupt clinical work.

--------------------------------------------------

19. Intent Flow (Search)

Oncologist opens the Intent Bar.

↓

Expresses intent, including searching for a patient.

↓

Select patient.

↓

Descend into Patient Space.

The Intent Bar is always available and replaces menu-based navigation. Patients may search their own records only.

--------------------------------------------------

20. Empty States

No reports uploaded.

Display upload prompt.

No tasks assigned.

Display informative message.

No notifications.

Display empty notification state.

No notes available.

Display empty notes state.

No visualization available.

Display informative message.

--------------------------------------------------

21. Error States

Failed login.

Incorrect credentials.

Failed upload.

Network error.

Session expired.

Space not found.

Permission denied.

Every error displays a clear message and provides a way to continue. An error inside one space never breaks the surrounding environment.

--------------------------------------------------

22. Sign Out Flow

User selects Sign out.

↓

Confirmation.

↓

Session ends.

↓

Return to Entry.

Sign out is a continuous ascent out of the environment.

--------------------------------------------------

23. Navigation Rules

Navigation is spatial and remains consistent throughout the environment.

The Contextual Orbit presents the same destinations in the same order for a given space.

Icons and labels remain consistent.

The current depth and current Patient Case are always identifiable.

Users always know where they are and where they came from.

Continuous Return always moves one depth level out.

Every important piece of information is reachable within three interactions.

Back navigation never loses unsaved work.

There is no persistent sidebar of navigation links and no nested menus.

All workflows remain simple and predictable.
