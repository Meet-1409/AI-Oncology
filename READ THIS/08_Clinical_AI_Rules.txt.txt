08_Clinical_AI_Rules.txt

Project Name

AI Oncology Patient Intelligence Platform

Document Purpose

This document defines how Artificial Intelligence behaves throughout the platform. Every AI service, AI model, and AI-generated output must follow these rules. AI exists to assist the oncologist by organizing and presenting information. AI never replaces clinical judgement.

--------------------------------------------------

1. Purpose of AI

The AI exists to assist the oncologist.

The AI helps organize patient information.

The AI helps summarize reports.

The AI helps compare reports.

The AI helps build the patient's clinical timeline.

The AI helps visualize disease progression.

The AI never makes medical decisions.

--------------------------------------------------

2. AI Responsibilities

Read uploaded reports.

Classify reports.

Extract structured information.

Generate report summaries.

Compare reports over time.

Generate patient intelligence.

Support the Digital Twin.

Support timeline generation.

Identify report relationships.

Provide supporting evidence.

Provide confidence scores.

--------------------------------------------------

3. AI Limitations

AI must never diagnose a patient.

AI must never prescribe treatment.

AI must never recommend medications.

AI must never replace the oncologist.

AI must never change uploaded reports.

AI must never hide original information.

AI must never modify patient history.

AI must never generate unsupported medical information.

--------------------------------------------------

4. AI Data Sources

AI only uses approved patient data.

Approved data includes:

Uploaded Reports

Medical Images

Laboratory Reports

Patient Information

Clinical Notes (if permitted)

Timeline Information

No external medical information should be mixed with patient data unless explicitly approved.

--------------------------------------------------

5. AI Processing Flow

Patient uploads report.

↓

Backend validates report.

↓

AI receives report.

↓

AI processes report.

↓

Structured information generated.

↓

Summary generated.

↓

Evidence linked.

↓

Confidence calculated.

↓

Patient Intelligence updated.

↓

Timeline updated.

↓

Digital Twin updated.

--------------------------------------------------

6. AI Report Classification

AI should identify the report type.

Examples include:

Pathology Report

MRI Report

CT Report

PET Report

Blood Test

Prescription

Discharge Summary

Surgery Report

Follow-up Report

Every report should be linked to the correct Patient Case.

--------------------------------------------------

7. AI Information Extraction

AI should extract available information including:

Cancer Type

Primary Site

Metastatic Sites

Tumor Stage

Tumor Size

Affected Organs

Treatment Information

Investigation Dates

Clinical Findings

Laboratory Values

Extract only information present in the uploaded data.

--------------------------------------------------

8. AI Report Summaries

Every summary should be:

Short

Clear

Clinically relevant

Easy to understand

Summaries must not replace the original report.

The original report should always remain available.

--------------------------------------------------

9. AI Report Comparison

Compare previous and current reports.

Identify changes.

Identify progression.

Identify regression.

Identify stable findings.

Highlight differences clearly.

Do not interpret findings beyond available evidence.

--------------------------------------------------

10. Patient Intelligence

Generate a structured overview of the patient's condition.

Present:

Current Disease State

Disease History

Recent Changes

Recent Treatments

Recent Investigations

Current Timeline

Supporting Evidence

Confidence

--------------------------------------------------

11. Digital Twin Support

Provide structured information required for the Digital Twin.

Provide:

Affected Organ

Disease Location

Disease Severity

Timeline Changes

Comparison Data

The AI provides only data.

The frontend renders the visualization.

Every organ-level result must carry its supporting evidence and confidence, so the frontend can reveal them when an organ is selected.

Results should be provided for every available clinical date, so movement through time remains continuous.

--------------------------------------------------

12. Timeline Support

Generate chronological events.

Every event should include:

Date

Event Type

Related Report

Related Evidence

Events should never be reordered incorrectly.

--------------------------------------------------

13. Evidence

Every AI result must include supporting evidence.

Evidence should point back to the original report.

Doctors should always be able to verify AI outputs.

Evidence must be structured so the frontend can present it directly alongside the statement it supports, rather than in a separate area. Verification must never require the doctor to leave their current space.

--------------------------------------------------

14. Confidence

Every AI result must include a confidence value.

Confidence should represent how certain the AI is based on available information.

Confidence should never be hidden.

--------------------------------------------------

15. Error Handling

If information cannot be extracted:

Do not guess.

Do not generate missing information.

Clearly indicate unavailable information.

Allow the oncologist to review the original report.

--------------------------------------------------

16. Explainability

Every AI output should answer:

What information was found?

Where was it found?

Which report contains it?

Why was this result generated?

Doctors should always understand how the AI reached its output.

--------------------------------------------------

17. Patient Safety

Patient safety is the highest priority.

AI must always preserve original medical information.

AI must never overwrite reports.

AI must never remove historical information.

AI must never hide conflicting information.

--------------------------------------------------

18. Future AI Models

Future AI models should follow these same rules.

New models should integrate without changing existing workflows.

Existing AI behavior should remain consistent.

--------------------------------------------------

19. Development Rules

AI services should remain independent.

AI should communicate only through backend APIs.

AI should not communicate directly with the frontend.

AI outputs should remain structured.

Every AI output should remain traceable.

--------------------------------------------------

20. Completion Criteria

The AI module is complete only when:

Reports are classified correctly.

Structured information is extracted.

Summaries are generated.

Comparisons are generated.

Patient Intelligence is generated.

Timeline information is generated.

Digital Twin data is generated.

Evidence is linked.

Confidence is displayed.

Original reports remain unchanged.

The oncologist can verify every AI-generated result.
