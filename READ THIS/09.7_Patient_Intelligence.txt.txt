09.7_Patient_Intelligence.txt

Project Name

AI Oncology Patient Intelligence Platform

Feature Name

Patient Intelligence

Document Purpose

This document defines the Patient Intelligence module. This module combines all validated patient information into a single clinical overview. It helps the oncologist understand the patient's condition without manually reviewing every report first.

--------------------------------------------------

1. Purpose

Patient Intelligence provides a complete overview of the patient's current clinical condition.

It summarizes the patient's journey using validated information while keeping every AI output linked to its original evidence.

The original reports always remain the source of truth.

--------------------------------------------------

2. Target User

Oncologist

--------------------------------------------------

3. Objectives

Provide a complete patient overview.

Display AI-generated clinical summary.

Display disease progression.

Display treatment progression.

Display supporting evidence.

Display confidence.

Provide quick access to related reports.

18. Business Rules

Every Patient Intelligence result must be generated from validated patient information.

Every AI output must be linked to supporting evidence.

Every AI output must display confidence.

The original report must always remain accessible.

Patient Intelligence must never replace the original reports.

21. Performance Requirements

Patient Intelligence should load quickly.

Large patient histories should remain responsive.

Search and filtering should update efficiently.

Opening related reports should not interrupt the current workflow.

22. Accessibility
