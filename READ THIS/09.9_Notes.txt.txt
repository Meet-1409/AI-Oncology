Project Name

AI Oncology Patient Intelligence Platform

Feature Name

Notes

Document Purpose

This document defines the Notes module. The module allows oncologists to write notes related to a patient's care. There are two types of notes: Patient Notes and Private Clinical Notes. Every note remains linked to the Patient Case.

--------------------------------------------------

1. Purpose

The Notes module allows oncologists to document important information throughout the patient's treatment journey.

Patient Notes are written for the patient.

Private Clinical Notes are written only for the oncologist.

--------------------------------------------------

2. Target Users

Patient

Oncologist

--------------------------------------------------

3. Objectives

Allow oncologists to create notes.

Allow patients to read patient notes.

Allow oncologists to maintain private clinical notes.

Maintain complete note history.

Link every note to the patient's timeline.

Oncologist Timeline

--------------------------------------------------

16. Business Rules

Only oncologists can create notes.

Patients cannot create notes.

Patients cannot edit notes.

Patients cannot delete notes.

Private Clinical Notes are never visible to patients.

Every note remains permanently linked to the Patient Case.

Every note keeps its creation history.
