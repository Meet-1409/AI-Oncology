09.3_Patient_Profile.txt

Project Name

AI Oncology Patient Intelligence Platform

Feature Name

Patient Profile

Document Purpose

This document defines the Patient Profile page. It serves as the central location for all patient information. Every report, timeline event, AI insight, Digital Twin visualization, task, and note originates from the Patient Profile.

--------------------------------------------------

1. Purpose

The Patient Profile provides the oncologist with a complete overview of the patient.

It combines patient information, medical history, reports, timeline, AI insights, and disease visualization into a single workspace.

--------------------------------------------------

2. Target User

Oncologist

--------------------------------------------------

20. Business Rules

Each Patient Profile belongs to only one patient.

Only the assigned oncologist can access the Patient Profile.

Patients cannot edit medical information.

Patients cannot modify reports.

Private Clinical Notes are never visible to patients.

Original reports must never be modified.

--------------------------------------------------

21. Performance Requirements

Patient information should load quickly.

Large report collections should support pagination.

Timeline should load efficiently.

Digital Twin should open without blocking the rest of the page.

--------------------------------------------------

22. Accessibility
