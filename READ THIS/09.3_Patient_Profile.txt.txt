09.3_Patient_Profile.txt

Project Name

AI Oncology Patient Intelligence Platform

Feature Name

Patient Space (Patient Profile)

Document Purpose

This document defines Patient Space. It is the central location for all patient information. Every report, timeline event, AI insight, disease visualization, task and note belongs to this space.

--------------------------------------------------

1. Purpose

Patient Space gives the oncologist a complete understanding of one patient.

It combines patient information, medical history, reports, timeline, AI insights and disease visualization into a single continuous environment at Depth 2.

It is not a profile page with tabs. It is a space the oncologist occupies, with the Body at its centre.

--------------------------------------------------

2. Target User

Oncologist

--------------------------------------------------

3. Objectives

Display patient information.

Display medical history.

Display diagnosis.

Display current treatment.

Provide quick access to reports.

Provide quick access to the timeline.

Provide quick access to Patient Intelligence.

Provide quick access to the Digital Twin.

Provide quick access to tasks.

Provide quick access to notes.

--------------------------------------------------

4. Entering Patient Space

Patient List or Intent Bar

↓

Select Patient

↓

Patient Space

The selected patient transforms into the space entered. Patient identity remains visible at all times.

Moving to another patient is a lateral movement and remains continuous.

--------------------------------------------------

5. Spatial Composition

The Body sits at the centre of Patient Space and shows the patient's current clinical state on arrival.

Patient identity and essential clinical context remain persistently visible.

The Contextual Orbit surrounds the Body and provides:

Patient Information

Journey (Timeline)

Evidence (Reports)

Understanding (Patient Intelligence)

Actions (Tasks)

Guidance (Notes)

The Journey is available as a continuous time control along the space. Moving through it moves the entire Patient Space through time, including the Body.

Selecting any destination reveals it alongside the Body rather than replacing the space. The Body always remains present.

19. Motion

Entering Patient Space uses spatial zoom from the selected patient.

Selecting a destination in the Contextual Orbit reveals it with motion that preserves the position of the Body.

Moving through the Journey updates the Body continuously rather than in discrete jumps.

Continuous Return zooms back out to Practice Space.

Reduced motion replaces spatial transitions with immediate cross-fades without any loss of information.

--------------------------------------------------

21. Business Rules

Each Patient Space belongs to only one patient.

Only the assigned oncologist can access the Patient Space.

Patients cannot edit medical information.

Patients cannot modify reports.

Private Clinical Notes are never visible to patients.

Original reports must never be modified.

--------------------------------------------------

22. Performance Requirements

Patient information should load quickly.

Large report collections should support pagination.

Timeline should load efficiently.

The Digital Twin should load without blocking the rest of the space.

Entering a previously visited Patient Space should not require a full reload.

--------------------------------------------------

23. Accessibility

Support keyboard navigation. Every spatial action has a keyboard equivalent.

Maintain readable typography.

Maintain proper spacing.

Provide descriptive labels for interactive elements.

Provide a non-3D equivalent for all Body content.

Respect reduced motion.

--------------------------------------------------

24. Completion Criteria

Patient Space is complete when:

Patient information is displayed.

Medical history is displayed.

Reports are accessible.

Timeline is accessible.

Patient Intelligence is accessible.

Digital Twin is accessible.

Tasks are accessible.

Notes are accessible.

Navigation follows the approved spatial model.

The space is ready for backend integration.
