Project Name

AI Oncology Patient Intelligence Platform

Document Purpose

This document explains what the product is, who will use it, what problems it solves, the features included in the first version, and the future direction of the platform.

--------------------------------------------------

1. Product Overview

The AI Oncology Patient Intelligence Platform is designed to help oncologists understand a patient's complete cancer journey from a single platform.

Patients usually receive treatment from multiple hospitals, laboratories, and diagnostic centres. As a result, their reports are scattered across different locations and are difficult to review during follow-up consultations.

This platform brings all patient reports together, organizes them, creates a complete timeline, and provides AI-assisted insights to help the oncologist understand the patient's condition more efficiently.

The platform is experienced as a continuous environment rather than a set of pages. Users move through it. The patient's body sits at the centre of that environment, and all clinical information is reached from it.

The platform does not replace the oncologist. It only assists in organizing and presenting clinical information.

--------------------------------------------------

2. Problem Statement

Cancer patients undergo multiple investigations throughout their treatment.

These may include:

- Pathology Reports
- CT Scan Reports
- MRI Reports
- PET Scan Reports
- Blood Tests
- Biopsy Reports
- Surgery Notes
- Discharge Summaries
- Prescriptions

These reports are usually generated at different hospitals and over long periods of time.

During follow-up visits, oncologists often need to review many reports before understanding the patient's current condition.

This process is time consuming.

Important information may be difficult to locate quickly.

Existing healthcare software makes this worse by requiring doctors to remember where information lives and to navigate through menus, boards and panels to reach it.

The platform solves this problem by organizing every report into one intelligent patient record, and by presenting that record as an environment where relevant information reveals itself in context.

7. Digital Twin

Every patient has an interactive Digital Twin.

The Digital Twin represents the patient's body using available patient information.

It is not intended to be an exact physical replica.

The Digital Twin is the centrepiece of the product and one of its primary navigation spaces.

The Digital Twin should:

Display disease location.

Display disease severity.

Display disease progression.

Support rotation.

Support zoom.

Support pan.

Support timeline transitions.

Support comparison between previous and current patient states.

Display supporting evidence.

Reveal the clinical information related to an organ when that organ is selected.

Integrate directly with reports, timeline and patient intelligence.

Remain responsive at all times.

--------------------------------------------------

8. Artificial Intelligence

The AI should:

Read reports.

Organize reports.

Summarize reports.

Compare reports.

Build patient timelines.

Identify disease progression.

Generate patient intelligence.

Display supporting evidence.

Display confidence.

AI must never replace medical judgement.

AI results are presented in context, next to the clinical information they describe, rather than in a separate reporting area.

--------------------------------------------------

9. Patient Workflow

Patient logs in.

Patient views assigned tasks.

Patient uploads requested reports.

Patient views upload status.

Patient views personal timeline.

Patient reads oncologist notes.

Patient updates profile if required.

Patient logs out.

The patient should never feel overwhelmed at any point in this workflow.

--------------------------------------------------

10. Oncologist Workflow

Oncologist logs in.

Oncologist opens assigned patient.

Oncologist reviews patient profile.

Oncologist reviews AI summary.

Oncologist reviews timeline.

Oncologist reviews Digital Twin.

Oncologist reviews uploaded reports.

Oncologist assigns patient tasks.

Oncologist writes patient notes.

Oncologist writes private clinical notes.

Oncologist saves updates.

Oncologist logs out.

The oncologist should never have to search for information. Relevant information reveals itself in context.

--------------------------------------------------

11. Reports

Patients upload reports.

Original reports remain unchanged.

Reports remain permanently stored.

Reports are organized automatically.

Reports become part of the patient's timeline.

Previous reports remain available.

--------------------------------------------------

12. Patient Management

Each patient is assigned to one oncologist.

The oncologist manages the patient's case.

The oncologist assigns tasks.

The patient completes assigned tasks.

The oncologist reviews uploaded information.

--------------------------------------------------

13. Notes

Patient Notes

Visible to the patient.

Written in simple language.

Used for instructions and follow-up.

Private Clinical Notes

Visible only to the oncologist.

Used for clinical observations.

Never shown to the patient.

--------------------------------------------------

14. Security

Secure authentication.

Role-based access.

Encrypted patient data.

Activity logging.

Audit history.

Secure file storage.

Healthcare-level privacy.

--------------------------------------------------

15. Experience Requirements

The first screen must immediately communicate product identity, quality and trust.

The entry experience must create curiosity and be memorable.

Navigation must feel continuous. Sudden page changes are avoided.

Motion must communicate movement through the system and explain where the user is.

Three-dimensional design is part of the product identity and is not limited to the Digital Twin.

The interface must feel premium, modern, elegant, confident, minimal and medical.

The interface must never be flashy, childish or game-like.

The application must not imitate existing EMR software.

Usability is never sacrificed for visual effect.

--------------------------------------------------

16. Out of Scope

The following features are not included in the first version.

Doctor-to-doctor chat.

Internal messaging.

Tumor board discussions.

Doctor collaboration.

Hospital management.

Appointment scheduling.

Billing.

Payment systems.

Inventory management.

Electronic prescriptions.

Video consultations.

Hospital administration.

--------------------------------------------------

17. Future Scope

Future versions may include additional AI capabilities and imaging features.

Future additions must follow the Ground Rules document.

Future features must not change the existing product vision.

--------------------------------------------------

18. Success Criteria

Patients can upload reports successfully.

Oncologists can review complete patient history.

Reports are organized automatically.

The timeline is generated correctly.

The Digital Twin displays disease progression.

AI summaries include supporting evidence.

Patient tasks function correctly.

Patient notes function correctly.

The platform remains secure, responsive, and easy to use.

A new user can complete their primary workflow without training.

Every important piece of information is reachable within three interactions.

The environment remains smooth and continuous during navigation.

--------------------------------------------------

19. Product Vision

Build a secure AI-powered platform that gives every oncologist a complete, organized, and easy-to-understand view of a patient's cancer journey while allowing patients to manage and submit their medical information from one place.

The experience should feel years ahead of existing healthcare software while remaining practical for daily clinical use.
