09.4_Report_Management.txt

Project Name

AI Oncology Patient Intelligence Platform

Feature Name

Evidence (Report Management)

Document Purpose

This document defines the complete Report Management module. It covers report upload, report organization, report viewing, report processing status, report comparison and report history.

--------------------------------------------------

1. Purpose

Evidence is the central repository for every medical report uploaded by the patient.

It allows patients to upload reports and allows oncologists to review, search, filter, compare and organize reports throughout the patient's cancer journey.

Evidence is what every AI result and every visualization traces back to. It is always reachable from the information it supports.

5. Spatial Composition

Evidence is reached from the Contextual Orbit in Patient Space, from an organ on the Body, or from an event on the Journey.

Reports are presented as a continuous, browsable set rather than a grid of cards.

Opening a report enters Focus. The surrounding space remains visible behind it, so the oncologist never loses their position.

Where Evidence is reached from an organ or an event, it arrives already scoped to that context, without requiring the user to filter manually.

18. AI Integration

After upload:

Backend stores report.

AI processes report.

Results become available.

Patient Intelligence updates.

Timeline updates.

Digital Twin updates.

These updates are revealed with motion so the oncologist notices what changed.

--------------------------------------------------

19. Motion

Opening a report lifts it into Focus above the current space.

Closing a report returns it to its position in the surrounding space.

Newly processed reports are revealed with motion that draws attention to the change.

Reduced motion replaces these transitions with immediate cross-fades without any loss of information.

21. Business Rules

Patients can upload reports.

Patients cannot edit uploaded reports.

Patients cannot delete uploaded reports.

Original reports remain unchanged.

Reports remain permanently linked to the Patient Case.

Every uploaded report becomes part of the patient's permanent medical history.

24. Performance Requirements

Large report collections should support pagination.

Search should remain responsive.

--------------------------------------------------

25. Accessibility

Support keyboard navigation. Opening, closing and comparing have keyboard equivalents.

Maintain readable typography.

Provide descriptive labels for every action.

Support screen readers where applicable.

Processing status must never rely on color alone.

Respect reduced motion.
